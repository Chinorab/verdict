How a trial runs

  1. Scan the label

    Each printed claim is matched to something a camera can measure. Claims it can’t check are left out, and we say so.

  2. Photograph on schedule

    Two photos on day 0 set your noise floor. Checkpoints are placed so the verdict lands before the deadline.

  3. Keep or return

    A result for each claim, a clear suggestion, and the refund at stake. The decision stays yours.

Every reading wobbles. We show by how much.

Light, angle and the camera shift a measurement a little every time. Your two day 0 photos measure that wobble. A change counts only when it is more than 1.5 times bigger.
